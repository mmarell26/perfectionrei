  • 5–50 unit apartment buildings

Our review process

1. Send the property

Provide the address, price guidance, unit count, occupancy, rents, expenses, condition, and any available supporting material.

2. Preliminary screening

We compare the opportunity against location, basis, operations, financing, and value-creation criteria.

3. Follow-up and diligence

If the property fits, we request the information needed for deeper underwriting and discuss next steps with the owner or broker.
