Acquisition criteria

Our multifamily buy box

A clear screen for owners, brokers, lenders, and partners who want to know what belongs in our pipeline.

Property Type

  • Multifamily / apartment buildings
  • Primarily 5–50 units
  • Mixed-use considered when residential is the primary value driver

Geography

  • Philadelphia
  • Suburbs and nearby Pennsylvania markets
  • Generally within roughly one hour of Philadelphia

Asset Profile

  • Value-add
  • Stabilized
  • Vacant or under-occupied
  • Deferred maintenance
  • Operational upside

Deal Sources

  • Owner-direct
  • Broker-represented
  • On-market
  • Off-market
  • Relationship-driven opportunities

Structures

  • Conventional acquisitions
  • Bridge or private financing where appropriate
  • Seller financing considered
  • Joint venture structures considered case by case

What Matters Most

  • Basis relative to current income and condition
  • Realistic path to stabilization or improvement
  • Market demand
  • Financing feasibility
  • Clear ownership and transaction path
Pricing

No artificial purchase-price ceiling.

We underwrite opportunities based on property-level economics, financing, risk, and the capital required to execute the plan. Size, basis, income, condition, and location matter more than a single headline price.

For the fastest preliminary review, send:

  • Property address
  • Asking price or price guidance
  • Unit count and current occupancy
  • Rent roll or current rents
  • Annual operating expenses
  • Taxes and owner-paid utilities
  • Known condition issues
  • Photos and offering memorandum, if available
