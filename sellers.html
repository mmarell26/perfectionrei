Owners can send the property directly to acquisitions and discuss timing, condition, tenancy, and transaction goals without unnecessary layers.

Closing timing depends on diligence, title, financing, documentation, and the individual transaction. We discuss realistic timing rather than making blanket guarantees.

What happens next

A simple owner-direct process

  1. Submit the property and basic operating information.
  2. We perform a preliminary screen.
  3. If it fits, we request supporting documents and discuss your objectives.
  4. Any potential terms remain subject to diligence, title, financing, and documentation.
