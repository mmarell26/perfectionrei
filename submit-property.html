Submit a property for review

Send the information you have. Missing items do not automatically disqualify a property, but complete operating information helps us screen opportunities more efficiently.

What happens after submission?

  1. We screen the location, unit count, basis, operations, condition, and available deal structure.
  2. If the opportunity appears to fit, we follow up for any missing underwriting information.
  3. Further discussion may include access, diligence, title, financing, timing, and potential terms.

Submitting a property does not create an obligation to purchase or sell. Any potential transaction remains subject to underwriting, diligence, financing, documentation, and approval.

Tip: A current rent roll, T-12 or expense summary, taxes, utility responsibility, photos, and an OM make preliminary review faster.
